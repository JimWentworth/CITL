BEFORE YOU TURN IT ON

AI Companion changes the meeting.

Meeting questions use speech-to-text data and prompts. Meeting summaries can also use speech-to-text data and, when enabled, shared-screen text and in-meeting chat. Treat activation as a facilitation and information-handling decision.

USEFUL SHORTCUT

Say the recap out loud.

AI Companion summarizes what it can hear. Reserve the final minute for a structured verbal recap, then ask the group to correct it. This gives the summary a clean source passage and gives people one last chance to resolve ambiguity.

60-SECOND RECAP

Decision: What did we agree?

Owner: Who will act?

Date: When is it due?

Open: What remains unresolved?

REVIEW BEFORE SHARING

A fluent summary can still be wrong.

Zoom advises users to check AI-generated content for accuracy. At Illinois, AI Companion also carries university-records considerations, so treat the output as shareable institutional content—not private scratch notes.

01

People & notice

Confirm participants knew AI was active and the intended use and audience were appropriate.

02

Decisions & context

Check that decisions are not confused with proposals, questions, or ideas that were rejected.

03

Owners & dates

Verify every name, action item, deadline, and follow-up against what people actually agreed to.

04

Records & sharing

Remove sensitive details, follow university and unit guidance, and share only with the intended audience.
