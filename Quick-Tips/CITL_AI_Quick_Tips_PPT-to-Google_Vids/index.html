USEFUL SHORTCUT

Write the narration in your speaker notes.

Add a short spoken script to each slide before converting. In the Narration step, choose Replace with speaker notes to use your prepared wording instead of starting from an AI-generated script.

SPEAKER NOTES PATTERN

Say the point.
Explain why it matters.
Give one concrete example.
Lead into the next scene.

HUMAN REVIEW

Treat the generated video as a first cut.

You remain responsible for the accuracy, media choices, accessibility, and viewing experience.

01

Accuracy

Verify claims, labels, names, and pronunciations.

02

Pacing

Give viewers enough time to read and absorb each scene.

03

Access

Correct captions; check contrast, text size, and visual clarity.

04

Media

Replace generic visuals and confirm permission to use each asset.

GOOD TO KNOW

AI-assisted conversion requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan. Without AI, you can still import selected slides as scenes.
