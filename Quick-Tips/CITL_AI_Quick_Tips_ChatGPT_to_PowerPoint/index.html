USEFUL SHORTCUT

REVIEW BEFORE SHARING

An editable deck is still a first draft.

ChatGPT can assemble and revise the presentation, but you own the argument, evidence, accessibility, and final visual judgment.

01

Story

Does every slide advance one clear idea for this audience?

02

Evidence

Do claims, numbers, charts, and quotations match the supplied sources?

03

Accessibility

Check reading order, contrast, type size, alt text, and descriptive links.

04

Brand & layout

Confirm templates, logos, spacing, cropping, and editable objects in PowerPoint.
