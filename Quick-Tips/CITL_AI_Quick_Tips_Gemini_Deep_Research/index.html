USEFUL SHORTCUT

The plan is your cheapest review point. Add missing stakeholders, require primary sources, narrow the date range, or remove irrelevant sections before Gemini spends time building the full report.

REVIEW BEFORE USING

A polished report can still be wrong.

Deep Research organizes the investigation. You remain responsible for checking its evidence, interpretation, and fitness for your purpose.

01

Authority

Prefer primary, expert, and institutionally credible sources.

02

Citation fit

Confirm each source supports the exact claim beside it.

03

Coverage

Look for missing perspectives, counterevidence, and gaps.

04

Currency

Check dates and re-verify claims that change quickly.
