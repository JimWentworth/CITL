An Audio Overview is an AI-generated interpretation of your selected sources. It is not comprehensive, objective by default, or automatically ready to publish.

Name what listeners already know and what they should understand afterward.

CUSTOMIZATION PROMPT

Give the hosts a producer’s brief.

Direct the conversation before generation instead of hoping the default episode finds the right emphasis.

PRODUCER NOTE

Create a [format] Audio Overview for [audience]. Focus on [two or three priorities]. Explain unfamiliar terms at a [expertise level] level. Compare the sources where they disagree. End with [desired takeaway]. Do not speculate beyond the selected sources.

✦

USEFUL SHORTCUT

Create separate episodes for separate audiences.

NotebookLM can store multiple Studio outputs in one notebook. Generate a short orientation for beginners, a detailed Deep Dive for specialists, or versions in different languages without rebuilding your source collection.

HUMAN REVIEW

Listen like an editor, not a fan.

The natural voices can make weak claims sound convincing. Review the substance as carefully as the sound.

01

Accuracy

Verify claims against the original sources and cited passages.

02

Coverage

Check what was omitted, oversimplified, or given too much weight.

03

Audio

Listen for pronunciation errors, speaker switches, glitches, or awkward pacing.

04

Rights

Confirm you can upload, transform, download, and share the source material.

SHARING NOTE

Workspace and Education accounts may not support public notebook links. You can still download the Audio Overview and share the audio through an approved course or media platform.
