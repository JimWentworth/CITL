WHAT EXPAND ACTUALLY DOES

It invents new pixels.
It does not recover the scene.

Firefly keeps the original image inside a larger frame and synthesizes content for the empty area. Treat that extension as a new visual claim—not as evidence of what was outside the camera’s view.

PROMPT RECIPE

Describe the seam,
not the whole image.

Tell Firefly how the new area should continue the existing scene and state what must not change.

CONTEXT + CONTINUITY + CONSTRAINT

Continue the [setting] with matching [light, color, texture, perspective]. Keep [subject or original feature] unchanged. Add [needed element]. No [unwanted objects, people, or text].

Leave the prompt blank when a natural continuation is all you need.

USEFUL SHORTCUT

Frame the destination first.

Choose the final aspect ratio before writing a prompt. Then position the original image inside that frame and generate only the space you actually need. This makes the prompt shorter and the result easier to evaluate.

EXAMPLE

Course banner · 16:9

SUBJECTSPACE FOR TITLE

Place the subject deliberately. Empty space can be a design feature.

REVIEW BEFORE USING

A seamless image can still be misleading.

Inspect technical quality and meaning. Pay extra attention when the original depicts people, real places, historical material, artwork, research, or evidence.

01

Edges & patterns

Zoom in for seams, repeated textures, warped lines, or abrupt changes where the original meets the extension.

02

People & objects

Check faces, hands, furniture, reflections, text, and recognizable objects for invented or malformed details.

03

Light & perspective

Confirm shadows, scale, horizon lines, depth, and color temperature continue believably.

04

Meaning & disclosure

Make sure the generated area does not misrepresent a real event, place, person, artwork, or research record.
