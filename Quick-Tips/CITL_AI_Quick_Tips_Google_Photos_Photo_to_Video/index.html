BEFORE YOU START

This feature requires a personal Google Account. It is not available to Google Workspace or school accounts, and availability can vary by region.

WHAT YOU NEED

01

The mobile app

Use Google Photos on Android or iOS with a network connection.

02

A backed-up photo

Choose a clear image with one obvious subject and some space around it.

03

An eligible account

You must be at least 13. Written motion prompts are initially limited to users 18 and older.

USEFUL SHORTCUT

Write the movement, not the makeover.

Describe what should move and how. Keep the subject, setting, and camera direction simple so the model has fewer decisions to invent.

MOTION PROMPT

“A light breeze moves their hair as they laugh together. Gentle handheld camera movement.”

SUBJECT ACTION＋ENVIRONMENT＋CAMERA

HUMAN REVIEW

Movement can change the meaning.

AI-generated clips are experimental. Check the result carefully before saving or sharing it.

01

Identity

Does the person, place, or object still look recognizably correct?

02

Motion

Do faces, hands, hair, and background movement look natural?

03

Consent

Do you have permission to animate and share the people shown?

04

Context

Could viewers mistake the generated action or audio for a real event?

GOOD TO KNOW

Google adds SynthID to generated photos and videos, plus a visible watermark on generated videos. Daily generation limits may apply.
