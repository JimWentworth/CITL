CUSTOM INSTRUCTIONS

Write a deck contract.

Make the success criteria visible before generation. Adjust the bracketed fields, then paste the brief into Slide Deck customization.

PASTE INTO THE CUSTOM PROMPT

Create a [Presenter Slides / Detailed Deck] for [audience] that helps them [purpose]. Use only the selected sources. Build [number] slides in this sequence: [outline]. Give each slide one clear claim, concise title, source-supported evidence, and a visual that explains the idea. Use [brand/style], high contrast, readable type, and minimal clutter. Include source attribution on relevant slides and a final references slide. Do not invent quotations, statistics, findings, or citations. Flag claims the sources do not support.

REVIEW BEFORE PRESENTING

Grounded does not mean finished.

Source selection narrows the evidence base; it does not guarantee that every generated statement, visualization, or design decision is accurate or appropriate.

01

Evidence

Verify every quotation, statistic, example, chart, and causal claim against the original source—not another generated summary.

02

Story

Check that the sequence fits the audience, each slide makes one clear contribution, and the ending delivers the intended action or insight.

03

Visual meaning

Confirm images and diagrams explain the content accurately and do not introduce stereotypes, false precision, or decorative noise.

04

PowerPoint access

Use unique slide titles, logical reading order, sufficient contrast, meaningful alt text, and PowerPoint’s Accessibility Checker.
