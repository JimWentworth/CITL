Summer AI Studio

The AI-enabled course studio: From ideation to integration

Launch Date June 15, 2026

In response to recommendations from the Campus AI Curriculum Task Force, the Provost’s Office has initiated a campus-wide Summer Intensive focused on integrating AI into the curriculum. This five-week program is designed to equip 40–60 faculty members with the skills to reflectively and responsibly integrate AI into their courses. Through hands-on workshops, collaborative design sessions, and expert guidance, faculty will redesign course components to incorporate AI in ways that enhance learning, critical thinking, and student engagement.

 

The program is being developed and delivered in collaboration with the Office of the CIO, the Center for Innovation in Teaching & Learning, the University Library, and AI experts from across campus. Courses were identified for participation based on their potential for high impact and opportunities to support student learning through thoughtful AI integration. More information about the task force and its recommendations is available on the Provost’s Campus AI Curriculum Task Force webpage.

The Teaching Package

At the conclusion of the Studio, each participant submits a structured Teaching Package. This serves as the roadmap for your Fall course.

1

Teaching Context

Course learning objectives and specific placement of the AI component within the semester schedule.

2

AI Integration Design

How students will use AI, tool/prompt selections, and the specific pedagogical support the AI provides.

3

Student Instructions

Ready-to-deploy, student-facing documentation for the assignment or activity.

4

Assessment Strategy

Clear criteria for evaluating student learning and AI-assisted outputs.

5

Transparency Policy

Explicit rules on how students must document and disclose their use of AI tools.
