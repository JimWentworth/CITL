AI Integration Design Document

Design and document a complete AI-integrated assignment or activity for your course.

What this is: Your submission documents a complete, deployable AI integration for your course — something you could actually use with students. It should cover your teaching context, how AI fits the design, the student-facing instructions, your assessment strategy, and your AI transparency policy. Upload a single Word or PDF document with all five sections clearly labeled.

Required components

1

Teaching context

  • Course name and number
  • Learning objective(s) addressed
  • Where this component fits in the course (e.g., week, unit, module)
2

AI integration design

  • How students will use AI
  • What prompts or tools they will use
  • What learning goal(s) the AI supports
3

Assignment / activity instructions

Full student-facing instructions, written and ready to deploy as-is in your course.

Note: this section should be addressed directly to students — written in the voice you'd actually use in your course. A colleague should be able to pick it up and use it without asking you any questions.
4

Assessment strategy

How student learning will be evaluated — describe how you'll know students did the thinking you intended, even with AI assistance.

5

Your AI use in designing this integration

Describe how you used AI tools in the process of designing this integration — what you asked, what it produced, and how you evaluated or modified what it generated. Be specific about where AI helped and where you made decisions it couldn't make for you.

Consider addressing
  • Which AI tool(s) you used and for what purpose
  • What prompts or inputs you gave the AI
  • How you evaluated, revised, or pushed back on what it produced
  • What the AI couldn't do — where your own judgment, expertise, or knowledge of your students was essential

Optional — student-facing materials

  • Links — to live tools, shared GPTs, Canvas pages, or hosted resources
  • Screenshots — showing the tool in use or the student-facing interface
  • Files — prompt templates, rubrics, or other downloadable resources

Links and screenshots can be included directly in your five-part document, or uploaded as separate files alongside it.
