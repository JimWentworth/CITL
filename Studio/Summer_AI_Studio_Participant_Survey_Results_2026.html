Q16

Sharing permissions

At the close of the survey, participants were asked whether their verbatim open-ended comments could be shared in reporting or with the Provost's Office, which sponsors the Studio.

Two respondents asked that their verbatim comments not be shared at all, even anonymously; a further twenty asked to be quoted only without their name attached. In line with those wishes, this page never attaches a name to any comment, and any comment from one of the two respondents who declined to share has been withheld from every "show all" list above rather than published without their consent — noted, where it occurred, as a response withheld by request.
