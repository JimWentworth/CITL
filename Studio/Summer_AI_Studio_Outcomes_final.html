Early feedback

Thirty Summer AI Studio participants rated the overall quality of the program's two halves: two weeks of in-person training, followed by three weeks of individual project work — the design documents cataloged in the Field Guide tab. Both halves scored well. Nobody rated either half "poor" or "very poor," and only a few rated either half merely "fair" (1 for the training weeks, 2 for the project weeks). The gap between the two means comes mostly from a "good" vs. "excellent" split: the in-person training pulled a slightly stronger share of top marks (15 of 30 "excellent"), while the individual project weeks skewed a bit more toward "good" than "excellent" (16 and 12).

Source: Q11 of the Summer AI Studio participant survey, "Please rate the overall quality of the two parts of the Summer AI Studio," n = 30. Figures are transcribed from the survey results export.
