Feedback Results

Sixteen Summer AI Studio participants rated the overall quality of the program's two halves: two weeks of in-person training, followed by three weeks of individual project work. Both halves scored well. Nobody rated either half "poor" or "very poor," and only one respondent rated each half merely "fair."

Source: Q11 of the Summer AI Studio participant survey, "Please rate the overall quality of the two parts of the Summer AI Studio," n = 16. Figures are transcribed from the early survey results on this one question.
